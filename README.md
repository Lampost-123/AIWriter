## Why AI Write

AI is good at writing a page. It is bad at remembering page one by the time it reaches page three hundred.
Names drift, the dead walk again, a locked door is suddenly open. AI Write keeps a memory of your story
beside the words, so that doesn't happen.

- **It remembers.** Every character, place, item, rule and plot thread lives in your world. Before each draft,
  the AI is given what matters for that scene: who is there, what they know, what they are wearing, what has
  already happened.
- **The memory looks after itself.** When a scene changes, AI Write reads it and updates the memory. It never
  overwrites what you typed, and every change it makes is listed with an **Undo**.
- **It checks your story.** Slips are caught as you write: a misspelt name, a fact that contradicts an earlier
  chapter, a character who knows something they shouldn't yet.
- **You stay the author.** The AI drafts, suggests and checks. You choose what stays. Every AI change can be
  accepted, rejected or undone, and you can write every word by hand if you like.
- **Your work lives on your computer.** Your worlds are ordinary folders in your Documents. AI Write has no account
  of its own and nothing to sign in to.

## Quick start

1. **Download** `AI-Write-Setup-<version>.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Lampost-123/AIWriter/releases/latest).
2. **Run it.** Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen, because the installer isn't code-signed yet.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. There's no admin prompt, and AI Write opens as soon as it's installed.
3. **Look round first, if you like.** Choose **Explore a sample world first** to open Gullhaven, a short finished story
   with its characters, places and memory filled in. Nothing in it costs anything.
4. **Connect an AI service.** We recommend **DeepSeek**: make a key at [platform.deepseek.com](https://platform.deepseek.com)
   and choose **DeepSeek** under the other services, or use an [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) key, which
   reaches DeepSeek and hundreds of other models. (Or choose **Skip for now**.)
5. **Pick a writer model.** We recommend **DeepSeek Flash**. Choose how your stories should read, and start writing.

The [Getting started guide](docs/guide/getting-started.md) walks through every step.

| **Optional: a graphics card** | Only for the read-aloud voices: an NVIDIA RTX 20-series or newer with 8 GB of memory. Dictation runs on any PC. |

**What costs money:** only the AI service you connect. You pay them for what you use. AI Write shows each
model's price before you pick it, and keeps a running total.

**What's free:** AI Write itself, the sample world, writing by hand, models you run on your own PC,
and read aloud, dictation and Find by meaning, which run on your computer.

## Your privacy

- **Your stories stay on your computer.** Worlds are kept in `Documents\AI Write` (you can move this folder
  in **Settings › About and updates**). Each world is a folder you can copy.
- **Only the AI service you choose sees your text**, and only what it needs for the job at hand: the scene,
  the parts of the memory that matter, and your style guide. **What the AI saw** shows you exactly what was sent.
- **Your keys are encrypted** with Windows' own protection and kept apart from your worlds. They never go into
  a world file, a backup or an export.
- **No tracking.** AI Write has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. Apart from your AI service,
  it connects to GitHub to check for updates, and downloads optional extras (voices, dictation, the search model)
  only for features that use them.

## FAQ

**Is AI Write free?**
Yes, the app is free. The AI service you connect charges for what you use. You can also run models on
your own PC with LM Studio or Ollama, which costs nothing per use.

**Which AI model should I use?**
We recommend **DeepSeek** models, especially **DeepSeek Flash**. It writes natural prose, copes well with long
stories, and is cheap enough to draft and redraft freely, for every job, not only the writer. Any other model
works too: the **Writer model** matters most, so pick one whose prose you like. See
[AI services and models](docs/guide/ai-providers.md).

**Will the AI change my writing without asking?**
No. AI edits appear as changes for you to accept or reject. The memory never overwrites what you typed into
an entry, and every change it makes can be undone.

**Can I bring in a book I've already written?**
Yes. **Import a manuscript…** reads Word, Markdown and plain text, splits it into chapters and scenes, and can
build the memory from it. See [Importing and exporting](docs/guide/importing-and-exporting.md).

**Does it work offline?**
Writing, editing, read aloud and dictation work offline. AI drafting and checks need your AI service, unless
you run a model on your own PC.

**Is there a Mac or Linux version?**
Not yet. AI Write is Windows only for now.

**How do I update?**
You don't need to do anything. AI Write checks for a new version when it starts, downloads it in the background,
and asks before restarting. Your worlds, settings and keys are kept.

**What happens to my work if I uninstall?**
Your worlds stay in `Documents\AI Write`. Reinstall any time and they're still there.

## For developers

AI Write is built with Electron, React, TypeScript and SQLite. You need Node 22.

```
npm ci             # install exactly what package-lock.json lists
npm run dev        # run the app with hot reload
npm run typecheck
npm test           # unit tests (Vitest)
npm run test:e2e   # end-to-end tests (Playwright)
npm run dist:win   # build the Windows installer
```

Read [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the layout and the rules, and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before opening a pull request.
