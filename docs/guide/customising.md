# Making it yours

AI Write should feel comfortable for long writing sessions. This page covers its look, the page itself, the editor,
your writing preferences, focus mode, and every keyboard shortcut.

## Choose a look

Open **Settings › Appearance**. Under **Style**:

- **New look** (the default): depth, colour and motion.
- **Classic**: flat and quiet, with everything in the binder.

In the New look, **Layout** chooses how the window is laid out:

- **Desk** (the default): the page in the middle, the story down a spine on the left, the rooms (Write, Plan, World,
  Check) in the top bar and the AI dock under the page.
- **Panels**: the areas down the left side, a list beside them, and the scene panel beside the page.

They all have the same features. Switch whenever you like.

## Theme and colour

In **Settings › Appearance**:

- **Theme**: **Match my computer**, **Light**, **Dark** (the default) or **Sepia**.
- **Accent colour**: **Theme colour**, **Teal**, **Indigo**, **Plum** or **Graphite**.

You can also switch theme from search (Ctrl+K): type "dark", "light" or "sepia".

If you've asked Windows for less motion (in Windows Settings, under Accessibility, Visual effects, by turning off
Animation effects), AI Write follows it.

## The page

Also in **Settings › Appearance**:

- **Text size**, **Line spacing** and **Page width** (how many characters fit on a line).
- **Paragraphs**: **Spaced** (a gap between paragraphs) or **Book** (indented, with no gap, as in a printed book).
- **When AI Write opens**: **The start screen** or **Where I left off**.

## The editor

In **Settings › Editor**:

- **Check spelling as I type**: right-click an underlined word for suggestions, or **Add to dictionary**. Names in
  your world always count as correct.
- **Smart punctuation**: curly quotes, and dashes and ellipses as you type. Press Ctrl+Z straight after to undo one.
- **Typewriter scrolling**: keeps the line you're writing at the same height on the screen.
- **Daily word target**: set **Words a day** for a daily goal, with a streak. Leave it empty for no target.

## Your writing preferences

**Settings › My writing preferences** holds your own defaults for every world: **Spelling** (UK or US),
**Point of view**, **Tense**, **Voice notes**, **Words to avoid**, and **Steer clear of common AI phrases**. Each world
and story can change them in its style guide (see [Planning](planning.md#set-the-style)).

## Focus mode

Press **F11** (or click **Focus mode** in the top bar) and only the page shows. Press Esc or F11 to leave.

You can also tidy the desk without leaving it: the arrow at the top of the story's spine folds it to a slim spine,
and **Scene details** shows or hides the scene panel. Search (Ctrl+K) for **Show or hide the binder** or
**Show or hide the scene panel** does the same.

## Find anything with search

Press **Ctrl+K** and start typing. Search finds scenes, chapters, entries and actions, such as "Export story",
"Check this chapter", "Dark theme" or "New scene". It's the quickest way to anything in AI Write.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** (when you're not typing in a box) to see this list in the app.

### Writing

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Generate a draft of the scene | Ctrl+G |
| Continue: the AI writes on from the end of the scene (on the desk) | Ctrl+Shift+Enter |
| Stop the draft | Esc |
| Accept the AI's change to the words | Tab |
| Reject the AI's change, or stop it while it's being written | Esc |
| Mark scene done | Ctrl+Enter |
| Save now (AI Write also saves as you type) | Ctrl+S |
| Bold / Italic | Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I |
| Quoted passage | Ctrl+Shift+B |
| Paste as plain text, without its formatting | Ctrl+Shift+V |
| New line in the same paragraph | Shift+Enter |
| Show who or what an underlined name is, beside the page | Ctrl+click |
| Listen from the cursor, or pause and carry on | Ctrl+L |
| Stop reading aloud | Ctrl+Shift+Space |
| Build the world from your summary (on the World builder page) | Ctrl+Enter |
| Focus mode (press again to leave) | F11 |
| Find and replace in this scene | Ctrl+F |
| Find and replace in the whole story | Ctrl+Shift+F |
| Undo / Redo | Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y |
| Dictation | The key you pick (hold, talk, let go) |

### Moving around

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Search, or find any action | Ctrl+K |
| This list of shortcuts | ? |
| Settings | Ctrl+, |
| Close a menu, list or dialog | Esc |
| Move between acts, chapters and scenes (in the binder) | Up / Down arrows |
| Close or open an act or chapter (in the binder) | Left / Right arrows |
| Open the scene (in the binder) | Enter |
| Rename an act, chapter or scene (in the binder) | F2 |
| Delete an act, chapter or scene (in the binder) | Delete |
| Bring back what you just deleted (in the binder or the Drafts tab) | Ctrl+Z |
