# Your world

Your world is everything the AI should remember: the people, the places, the history, the rules. Each thing has
its own page, called an entry. The AI reads the entries that matter before every scene, and the memory keeps them
up to date as you write.

You'll find it all under **World** on the left (in the **Classic** look, in the binder's **World** section).

<p align="center"><img src="../images/world-entry.png" alt="Wren Halloway's page in the sample world" width="800"></p>

## What a world holds

| Kind | For example, in Gullhaven |
|---|---|
| **Characters** | Wren Halloway, the keeper's daughter; Edric Halloway, keeper for forty years |
| **Places** | Gullhaven; The Gullhaven Light; The Drowned Steps, a causeway above water only at low tide |
| **Groups** | The Harbour Board, which pays the keeper |
| **Items** | Things that matter to the story, like the sealed letter |
| **Lore** | History, customs and rules, like "The light is never dark" |
| **Events** | Things that happened, placed on the timeline |
| **Plot threads** | Open questions, like "What is in the sealed letter?" |
| **Glossary** | Words and terms special to your world |

## Browse everything in the Codex

The **Codex** is "Everything the AI remembers about your world." Use **Search the codex** to find an entry, filter
by kind, tag, story or role, and change the order with **Sort by**. Click any entry to open its page.

## Add an entry by hand

1. Open the kind you want under **World** (for example, Characters).
2. Click **New character** (or **New place**, and so on).
3. Fill in what you know: the name, **Aliases** (other names the story uses), a **Short summary**, a
   **Description** and any tags. Leave the rest empty if you like.

Everything saves as you type.

> **Tip:** anything you type yourself is marked **You wrote this**. The memory will never change what you've
> written. It only adds what it reads from your story, marked **Read from your story**.

## Add a name straight from the page

Select a name in a scene and use the bar above it:

- **Add to memory** makes a new entry from the selected words.
- **Quick start a character** builds a whole character from the words around it (see below).

## Build a character with AI

The character builder works for characters, places, groups and items. Open it from an entry with
**Open in the builder**, or search (Ctrl+K) for **Quick start a character**.

### Quick start

1. Type a few notes, as much or as little as you like: "Wren, 19, the keeper's daughter. Stubborn, good with her
   hands, afraid of the sea."
2. Click **Build the character**. The AI fills in the rest. Your own words are kept exactly as you wrote them.
3. Read it through. Click **Finish the rest** to let the AI fill any gaps, or **Go step by step instead**.

### Step by step

The builder walks through **Basics**, **Looks**, **Personality**, **Backstory and secrets**, **Goals and arc**,
**Voice**, **Relationships** and a final **Review**.

- **Flesh out with AI** fills in a step. **Keep** or **Discard** each suggestion, or **Keep all**.
- **Options** beside a field gives three ideas for it. Click **Use this** on the one you like.

### Interview a character

**Interview** lets you talk to a character, and they answer in their own voice. It's a good way to find how they
speak. When they say something that sounds just right, click **Save as a sample line** and the AI uses it to keep
their voice.

### Add a portrait

On an entry's page, click the picture to choose an image, or drop one onto it.

## Build a world from a summary

If you already know your world, describe it and let the AI lay it out.

1. Under **World**, choose **Build from a summary**.
2. Type or paste your description into **Your summary**: a paragraph or a few pages.
3. Click **Build the world** (Ctrl+Enter).

The AI makes the characters, places, lore, events and plot threads it finds. It also lists what's
**Already in your world** (left as it is) and anything **Where your summary disagrees with your world** (listed as
a consistency issue for you to look at, with nothing changed). Changed your mind? **Undo the whole build**.

Don't know where to start? Click **Interview me** and the AI asks you questions about your world, one at a time.

## See an entry as it was at any point

Characters change. Wren may learn a secret in chapter three, and the AI shouldn't know it in chapter one.

1. Open an entry.
2. Click **View as of a scene**.
3. Move the slider to a scene. The page shows what was true at that point: what has happened so far, what the
   character knows, and what has changed.

Each entry also shows where it **First appears**, which scenes it **Appears in**, what it **Knows at the start**, and
how it **Changes over time**.

## Rules the AI must never break

For lore, turn on **Hard rule**: "Never break this rule. Always given to the AI." In Gullhaven, "The light is never
dark" is a hard rule, so the AI won't let the lighthouse go dark unless your story makes it happen.

## Keep private notes

Every entry has **Private notes (never sent to the AI)**, for ideas you're not ready to commit to.

- The **Relationship map** shows how your characters are tied to each other, and how each feels about it. Move the
  slider to see it at any point in the story.
- The **Timeline** shows scenes and events in the order they happen in your world. Dates come from the **When** box
  on each scene card. Choose **Lanes for** characters or plot threads. It warns you if a character is in two places
  at once.
- The **Plot threads board** shows every thread as **Open**, **Resolved** or **Planned**.

## Names in your scenes

Names from your world are underlined in the page. Rest the pointer on one to see a short card about it.
Ctrl+click a name to open its entry beside the page, without leaving the scene.

The **Cast** tab in the scene panel lists who's in the scene: the **Point of view** character, who's **Also in the
scene**, and who's **Named in the text**.

## Deleted something by mistake?

Every delete shows an **Undo** straight away. After that, deleted entries, scenes and chapters stay in
**Settings › Recently deleted** for 30 days, where you can restore them.
