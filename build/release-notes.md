This update fixes "Memory not updated" with models that think before they answer.

- Those models could spend all their room thinking and send nothing back. The memory now asks them not to think, and leaves room to answer if they think anyway.
- New: a Thinking choice for the writer and for the memory, in Settings › Models. Both start on Off.
- Scenes marked "Memory not updated" are read again by themselves.
