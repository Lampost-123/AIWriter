What's new in 0.6.44:

- A critic for your writing: the new Critique tab, beside Issues, gives honest feedback on a scene or a whole chapter when you ask.
- It looks at pacing, tension, voices and dialogue, clarity and the prose, and for a chapter its shape and ending.
- Click a note to see its words in the page, or press Rewrite this to get a rewrite to accept or reject.
- Critiques are kept, and say when the words have changed since.
