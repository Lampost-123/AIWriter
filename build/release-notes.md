What's new in 0.7.2:

- AI Write keeps track of who knows what: what each character has learned, since when and how, and what they wrongly believe. The writer is told for everyone in the scene, not just the point of view.
- Promises, clues and threats have a status and an optional "Pay off by" chapter. The writer is reminded of the ones due, and Worth a look asks about any left waiting too long.
- A story calendar works out each scene's date from its When, how long ago things happened, and how old people are, so the AI doesn't have to do the sums.
- Characters are spotted however the story names them, and new nicknames are offered in Worth a look.
- Free checks, with no AI, catch the dead speaking, one thing in two hands, and time running backwards. They stay quiet when the memory isn't sure.
- Each scene gets a contract: what must be true at the start, what must change, and what must not. The draft is checked against it, and you're asked before anything is rewritten.
- Before each scene the memory writes the writer a short continuity brief of what matters most. Both can be turned off in Settings › Models.
- Chapter summaries are rebuilt from the scenes and the record, never from older summaries, so they don't drift.
