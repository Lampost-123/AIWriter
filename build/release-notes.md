What's new in 0.6.46:

- Setup offers the read-aloud download. Characters get studio voices by sex and age.
- Read aloud skips “she said” after voiced lines.
- A critic for your writing: the new Critique tab, beside Issues, gives honest feedback on a scene or a whole chapter when you ask. Click a note to see its words, or press Rewrite this.
- Continue and the AI tools keep writing, and read aloud keeps reading, when you open another scene.
- Beat by beat's box no longer goes missing. An unfinished session comes back as it was, even after a restart.
